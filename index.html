EST. AN INHERITED ART OF CELESTIAL INTERPRETATION
01 Our approach

Not a prediction machine.
An interpretative tradition.

Astrology is part of a long intellectual history—shaped by observation, calculation, philosophy, and the symbolic interpretation of celestial phenomena.

Sphaera draws upon this inheritance with care and discernment. We look to the texts, techniques, and ideas developed across ancient and medieval traditions, bringing them into a thoughtful dialogue with contemporary life.

Consultations are offered as considered interpretations—not certainties, prescriptions, or substitutes for your own judgment.

02 Historical foundations

Many cultures.
One enduring inquiry.

The study of the heavens developed across civilisations, with knowledge translated, challenged, refined, and transmitted over centuries.

I

Mesopotamia

Celestial observation, astronomical records, omen traditions, and the early systematic study of planetary cycles.

II

Egypt

Stellar timekeeping, decans, calendars, and conceptions of celestial and terrestrial order.

III

Greece

Mathematical astronomy, philosophical cosmology, and the systematic development of horoscopic astrology.

IV

Rome

Astrology’s literary, practical, political, and philosophical reception in the Roman world.

V

Arabic-speaking scholars

Translation, preservation, calculation, critique, and important developments in astrological and astronomical learning.

These traditions are distinct and complex; their history is not a single uninterrupted doctrine. Sphaera aims to recognise their differences as well as their transmission.

06 Questions

Frequently asked.

What information is needed for a natal consultation?

Typically, date of birth, recorded birth time if available, and birthplace. The final ordering process will explain what is required and how to provide it appropriately.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Some techniques depend on a reliable birth time. We will explain which parts of the analysis can be undertaken and what limitations apply when the time is unknown or uncertain.

How will I receive my analysis?

Consultations are planned as personalised written reports delivered digitally, generally in PDF format. The delivery timeframe will be agreed before an order is accepted.

Is astrology a scientifically established predictive method?

No. Astrology is presented here as a historical and symbolic interpretative tradition, not as a scientifically validated method of prediction. Interpretations are not guarantees of events or outcomes.

Do you offer live consultations?

The initial service is planned around written reports. Live consultations may be considered in the future.
